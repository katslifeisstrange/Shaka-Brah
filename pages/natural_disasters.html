Tsunamis

Epi- and Hypocenter

Tsunamis have an Epicenter, aswell as a Hypocenter. The Hypocenter is where the collision of two earth plates happens, and the tsunami wave is triggered. The Epicenter is vertically in line with the Hypocenter. It's located on the surface of the earth, somewhere in an ocean. Tsnuamis happen mostly in the pacific ocean.

Speed

A regular Tsnuami wave travels the ocean at about 800km per hour. When it gets to shore, it slows down to somewhere between 8km and 50km per hour. This concludes that the deeper the water below the tsunami is, the faster it travels.

When does an earthquake turn into a tsunami?

But, when does an earthquake turn into a tsnumai? There are three determening factors for that. 1 - the magnitude has to be 7 or more. 2 - the Hypocenter has to be close to the surface. 3 - The earthquake causes a vertical shift of the ocean's floor, which puts the water into movement and finally, causes huge waves.
